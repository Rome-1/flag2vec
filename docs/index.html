Five things the encoder saw

    01

    Nordic crosses are visual twins.

    Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Iceland and Finland sit in the tightest cluster of any tradition — 64% k=5 nearest-neighbour purity in 384 dimensions. The structure carries the signal, not the colour: a single off-centre cross survives every palette it is painted in.

    02

    British ensigns crossed an ocean and stayed siblings.

    The UK, Australia, New Zealand, Fiji and Tuvalu share a canton-on-blue template, and despite wildly different fly designs the encoder files them together. Australian state flags pile in on top of them — a 31× k-NN lift over chance.

    03

    Tradition beats geography.

    Europe scatters across the whole map. Latin America does not: the horizontal tricolour with a central charge forms one tight subcluster spanning two continents. Argentina, Uruguay, Honduras, El Salvador — visual cousins, political coincidence.

    04

    Communist red doesn't cluster.

    China, Vietnam, North Korea, Belarus, Mongolia, Angola — a shared colour, not a shared structure. Because the encoder reads layout over palette, a red field with a star, a hammer-and-compass, an ornate seal and a black stripe end up nowhere near each other.

    05

    Mars inherits — sometimes.

    Twenty-five Martian regional flags, each procedurally derived from an Earth tradition. 13 of 25 land closest to the centroid they inherited from. Structurally distinctive parents (Nordic, solid+emblem, pan-Arab) pass the resemblance down; colour-only parents (heraldic, pan-African) do not.

How the map was made

Encoder DINOv2 ViT-S/14, frozen. Self-supervised on natural images; never fine-tuned, never shown a label.
Corpus 423 flags — 197 sovereign, 201 subdivision, 25 Martian. SVGs rasterized before encoding.
Alignment Two encoder runs (398 flags at 518 px, 197 at 224 px) joined into one 384-d frame by orthogonal Procrustes on the 197 shared sovereigns.
Projection 3D PCA · 3D t-SNE (perplexity 20, cosine) · 3D UMAP (n=15, min_dist=0.05).
Palette Four-colour median cut per flag, classified into twelve named families. Used for the palette colouring, never for the embedding.
Lineage A visual analogue of Figure 3 in emoji2vec. Sister project: music2vec.
